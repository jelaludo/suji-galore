# Rhombus-Segment B16 · 菱

A diamond-shaped variant of Quarter-Circle B16 and Square-Segment B16. Each quadrant has one beveled diagonal bar: upper left = 1, upper right = 2, lower left = 4, lower right = 8.

All four sides have equal length and meet at virtual right-angle tips, making a perfect rhombus (a rotated square). The tips remain open with generous gaps. The reference image supplies the outline; its central cross, labels, and equality marks are not part of the numeral.

Base-16 places nest inward from the largest units rhombus, with progressively smaller, thinner bars. Zero alone is a centre diamond; empty positional places have a short top marker. The variant shares the subtle CRT glow and stationary scanlines of Square-Segment B16 and supports all three Study modes for 0–15.
