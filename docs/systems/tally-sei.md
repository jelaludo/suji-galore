# 正 tally · 正の字

One 正 groups five events. A partial character shows the count’s history in canonical stroke order: top horizontal, centre vertical, middle horizontal, left vertical, then bottom horizontal. After five strokes, the next character begins.

This cumulative representation uses one drawn part per counted event. It therefore grows smoothly and predictably, though much less compactly than positional systems.
