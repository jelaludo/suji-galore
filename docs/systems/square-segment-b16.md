# Square-Segment B16 · 方

A square variant of Quarter-Circle B16 with the same quadrant weights: upper left = 1, upper right = 2, lower left = 4, lower right = 8.

Each bit consists of two filled, chamfered bars oriented at 90 degrees. Four active bits describe a perfect square with generous open corners and small midpoint gaps. Higher base-16 places form progressively smaller squares inside the units square; bar thickness also decreases inward.

Zero alone is a centre diamond. Empty positional squares use a short top marker. The display adds subtle stationary CRT scanlines and a soft glow, without flicker. All three skins and quadrant explanation colors remain available.

This is an original experimental notation, available in the gallery and Study modes alongside Quarter-Circle B16.
